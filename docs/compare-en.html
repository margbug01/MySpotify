HOW TO CHOOSE

Which client fits your workflow?

Choose MySpotify if…

You want a fast, non-Electron native GUI under 10 MB with built-in floating desktop lyrics, LRCLIB/NetEase fallback, music videos, podcast transcripts and zero telemetry—even on a free account.

Choose Official Spotify / Spicetify if…

You rely on Premium offline downloads, lossless audio, Jam, AI DJ, local files, or require strict official Terms of Service compliance.

Choose a TUI client if…

You already have Spotify Premium, live inside a terminal on Linux/macOS, and don't need cover art, floating lyrics, or music videos.

FAQ

Comparison FAQ

Why does MySpotify use less RAM than the official Spotify desktop client?

The official Spotify desktop app bundles CEF (Chromium Embedded Framework) to render its entire UI. MySpotify draws its UI natively with Go and MyGo, invoking the OS WebView2 or WebKit DRM module only for playback decryption—resulting in an installer under 10 MB, ~190 MB RAM while browsing, and ~60 MB extra while playing.

How does MySpotify compare to Spicetify?

Spicetify patches the official Spotify client with custom themes and JS extensions while keeping the official CEF runtime and offline downloads. MySpotify is a standalone native Go app that does not require the official client, uses far less disk and RAM, and ships built-in floating desktop lyrics, multi-source lyrics fallback and zero telemetry.

When should I stick with the official Spotify desktop app?

Use the official Spotify desktop app if you need offline downloads, lossless audio, Jam, AI DJ, friend activity, local audio files, or zero risk of violating Spotify's third-party client terms.
